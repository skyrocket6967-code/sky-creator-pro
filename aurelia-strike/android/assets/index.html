CONTROLS

TOUCH

Left thumb anywhere on the left: floating joystick. Push to the edge to sprint.

Right thumb: drag to look. Hold FIRE and drag to aim while shooting.

JUMP: tap to hop, hold in the air for the jetpack. Land on cover and containers.

AIM zooms (the Longshot uses a scope). Gyro aiming is in Settings.

CONTROLLER

RT fire · LT aim · A jump / jetpack · B dash

X reload · Y next gun · LB grenade · R3 toggle aim

D-pad cycle guns · Start pause. In menus: D-pad moves, A selects, B goes back.

KEYBOARD

WASD move · mouse look (double-click to lock) · Space jump · Shift sprint · C dash · R reload · G grenade · Q aim · 1–0 guns · Esc pause
